# Home page search

## 1. Goal

A store owner finds a product, or a customer's orders, from the Home page.

## 2. Users

The store owner, signed in to their store.

## 3. Home page

The page shows the five most recent active products of the store, each with its five newest orders. A search box above the list filters the products by title or by customer name as the user types. When nothing matches, the list shows "No products to show".

## 4. Out of scope

Sorting, filters other than the search, and paging.
